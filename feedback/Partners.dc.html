[ BECOMING A PARTNER ]

Four conversations, then we're running it.

There's no application window and no committee. We take on what we can support well, and we'll tell you within a week if that's not you.

01

Intro call

Thirty minutes. What you have, what's failing, who touches it.

02

Technical review

We look at the code, the data, and the hosting bill you're trying to escape.

03

Written agreement

Plain-language scope: what we cover, what we don't, how data is handled, how either side exits.

04

Migration

We move it, verify a restore, and hand you documentation. Usually under two weeks.
