# Delete Your Esperanza Account

#content

You can ask NPTS, the developer of the **Esperanza** app, to delete your account and its data. You do not need to have the app installed.

## How to request deletion

1. Send an email to [info@npts.tech](mailto:info@npts.tech) from the email address you use to sign in to Esperanza.
2. Use the subject line **Delete my Esperanza account**.
3. We will reply to confirm the request, and we will finish deleting the account within **30 days**.

If you no longer have access to that email address, write to us anyway and tell us your name and username. We will ask a few questions to confirm that the account is yours.

## What we delete

- Your account: name, email, username, profile picture, password and sign-in sessions.
- Your demographic profile.
- Your event RSVPs and the usage records linked to your account.
- If you are a provider: your organization profile. Your past events are removed or no longer linked to you.

## What we keep

- Summary reports that were made before deletion. They contain only counts and percentages and cannot identify you.
- Records we must keep by law, if any, only for as long as the law requires.

See the [Esperanza Privacy Policy](/privacy/esperanza) for more details.
