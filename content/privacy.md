# Privacy Policy

#content

_Effective date: October 6, 2026_

This policy explains how Non-Profit Technical Support ("NPTS", "we", "us") handles personal information. It covers this website (npts.tech) and the apps NPTS publishes, including apps on Google Play.

## Summary

- This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.
- We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.
- We collect only what we need to run our services, answer you and process donations.

## Information we collect

**When you visit npts.tech.** The site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address for security and to keep the service running. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). NPTS does not receive or store this data.

**When you email us.** If you write to info@npts.tech, we receive your email address and anything you include in the message. We use it only to reply to you.

**When you donate.** Donations are processed by Stripe on a Stripe-hosted page. Stripe collects your payment details under the [Stripe Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy). NPTS receives your name, email address, billing details and the amount and date of your donation. We never receive your full card number. We use this information to record your donation, send receipts and meet legal and tax obligations.

**When you use an NPTS app.** An app may ask for the information it needs to work, such as:

- account details, such as your name, email address and password;
- information you enter or upload in the app;
- your approximate location or an address you search for, if the app has map or directions features.

We use this information only to provide the app's features, keep your account secure and fix problems.

## App-specific policies

NPTS builds and publishes apps for partner organizations. Each app has its own privacy policy. It names the partner organization and lists exactly what that app collects, who can see it and how to delete your data. Where an app policy differs from this page, the app policy applies to that app.

- [Esperanza](/privacy/esperanza), built for the Institute of Urban Studies

## How we share information

We share personal information only:

- with service providers that host or run our services for us, such as our cloud hosting provider, Stripe and email providers, which may use it only to provide those services;
- with the non-profit partner organization an app was built for, when that is part of the service the app provides;
- when the law requires it, or to protect the safety of people or the security of our services.

## Data retention and deletion

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above. Donation records are kept for as long as tax law requires.

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal information, or to delete your app account and its data. Email info@npts.tech from the address linked to your account. We will reply within 30 days.

## Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), access controls and hosting on managed cloud providers to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify affected people if a breach occurs, as the law requires.

## Children

Our services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: [info@npts.tech](mailto:info@npts.tech)
