# Esperanza Privacy Policy

#content

_Effective date: October 6, 2026_

This policy covers the **Esperanza** community services app. Non-Profit Technical Support ("NPTS", "we", "us") builds, hosts and publishes Esperanza for the **Institute of Urban Studies**. NPTS is the developer named on the Google Play listing.

The general [NPTS Privacy Policy](/privacy) also applies. Where the two differ, this page applies to Esperanza.

## Who uses Esperanza

- **Residents** find community events and services, RSVP to events and manage their profile.
- **Providers** (social service organizations) post events and see summary reports about the residents they serve.
- **Administrators** from the Institute of Urban Studies and NPTS run the app.

## Information we collect

**Account information.** Your name, email address, optional username and profile picture, preferred language (English or Spanish), and your password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash.

**Demographic information.** When you fill in your profile, you may give your age or age range, gender, race, ethnicity, marital status, number and ages of children, county of residence, primary language, education, employment status and industry, personal and household income ranges, and whether you have a vehicle. You choose what to fill in.

**Activity in the app.** The events you RSVP to, and how you use the app, such as pages viewed and searches.

**Provider information.** If you are a provider, your organization name, description, logo, website, contact email and phone, and the events you post, including event locations.

**Technical information.** When you sign in, we store your IP address and device/browser type with your session to keep your account secure.

## How we use it

- To run your account and show you relevant events and services.
- To give providers and the Institute of Urban Studies **summary reports** about the people they serve, such as percentages by age range or county.
- To improve the app and fix problems.
- To keep the app secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Who can see your information

**Providers.** The "Share profile information with providers" setting controls this. It is on by default, and you can turn it off at any time in your profile. When it is on, your demographic information is included in summary reports for providers whose events you RSVP to. Your name and contact details are not shown in those reports.

**The Institute of Urban Studies.** It receives summary reports and can access app data as the organization the app is run for.

**NPTS administrators.** They can access app data only to operate, support and secure the app.

**Service providers that help run the app:**

- Amazon Web Services hosts the app and stores its data in the United States.
- Google Gemini translates event titles and descriptions written by providers between English and Spanish. Resident information is not sent to it.
- Map tiles come from OpenFreeMap. Address search for event locations uses Geoapify or Photon. These services receive your IP address and map or search requests, but no account information.

**Legal reasons.** We may share information when the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to the app's servers and database is limited to authorized NPTS administrators. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

## Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while your account is active. You can change or remove your profile and demographic information in the app at any time.

To delete your account and all data linked to it, see [Delete your Esperanza account](/delete-account/esperanza). We delete the account within 30 days of a verified request. Summary reports that were already made cannot be traced back to you and are kept.

## Children

Esperanza is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

We will post changes on this page and update the effective date. For major changes we will also notify users in the app.

## Contact

Questions or requests about Esperanza and your data: [info@npts.tech](mailto:info@npts.tech)
